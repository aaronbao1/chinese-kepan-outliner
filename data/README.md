# data/

| Dir / file | Contents | In git? |
|---|---|---|
| `raw/` | Fetched sources, bytes untouched. Each source has a `scripts/fetch_<source>.sh` (with `--check`) and sha256 lines in `scripts/CHECKSUMS`. Subdirectories: `cbeta/` (the 19 pinned works, `LICENCE-NOTICE.*`, and, when fetched, the `xml-p5/` and `xml-p5-2018/` corpus clones and the `survey/` tables), `cbeta-metadata/`, `cbeta-api/`, `dila-ybh/`, `dila-sdp/` (with `gold/`: the two eval-only sdp golds, their crosswalk and the heading check, built by `pipeline/src/chinese_workflow/eval/gold/sdp.py` and `sdp_check.py`) and `bdrc/`. About 5 GB with both corpus clones; the projects need only `cbeta/` (`scripts/quickstart.sh` fetches it). E00's CBETA API cache lives under `experiments/E00-kepan-census/out/api/` instead (regenerated by `census.py fetch`, not checksummed). | no |
| `reference-outlines/` | The committed gold outlines, laid out `<text-id>/<scheme_id>/`, each with a `PROVENANCE.md`: the Kuiji gold of T0262 (a draft), the CBETA 科判 golds of X0268, P1573 and D8842, and the YBh golds of T1605 and T1602 (`reference-outlines/README.md`). They are independent outlines under profile `zh-kepan` (`context/baseline/outline-schema-zh.json`). Only material whose licence allows redistribution goes here; sdp-derived golds stay under `raw/`. | yes |
| `processed/` | Pipeline outputs. The runner writes `data/processed/<project id>/` unless `--out` says otherwise; everything there is reproducible from `raw/` and a `pipeline/` commit. | no |
| `eval-sets.json`, `EVAL-SETS.md` | The eval-set registry and its guide: every gold, heading check, parser-case file and fixture with its path, anchors, licence, split membership, gaps and the experiments that use it, and the **dev / validation / test / reserve split spans** (the single source of truth for them). Counts and tables are computed by `scripts/eval_sets.py`; `tests/unit/test_gold_registry.py` checks them. The guide also holds the rules for gold data and the notes a scorer needs. | yes |

Reference-outline licence policy: a gold derived from CBETA is public only under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 with CBETA's notice and release label; a gold derived from DILA's YBh outlines under CC BY-SA 4.0; nothing is relicensed more permissively. `data/reference-outlines/NOTICE` carries the notices these licences require. So the golds fall into two groups: those from licensed sources (CBETA, YBh), committed under `reference-outlines/`, and those from sdp, which has no licence, kept eval-only under `raw/`.

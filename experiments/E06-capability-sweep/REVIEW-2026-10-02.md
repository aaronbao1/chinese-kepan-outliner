## 1. Bottom line

1. **The Zhiyi zero is three things, and the parser's formula rules are not one of them.** On T1718 dev the parser accepted 9 of 9 real division announcements and rejected 9 of 9 non-divisions (VERIFIED, `runs/tier2-T1718-dev/outline-report.json`, re-run of the scanner over the dev span). The 11 misses decompose as: 1 top-level convention miss (sdp opens a second level-1 node at `p0003a19`; the parser nests everything under the 品 pin and a wrapper), 2 same-line-and-depth-but-other-parent, 2 one level off, and 6 members of same-line chains (VERIFIED from `score-dev.json` per-depth and FN ids). The chains are sdp's convention: a node's key is its text-span start, so a parent and its first-child chain share one line (EVAL-SETS item 3). The 12 dev gold nodes sit on 5 distinct lines, which caps locator-only recall at 5/12 = 0.417 for any outliner that keys a node at its own take-up line (VERIFIED). REPORT's "detection finds one division in ten" is right about the number and wrong about the cause.
2. **What the parser does lack on Zhiyi is two non-formulaic devices.** (a) The quoted-lemma gloss 「X」者 / 「X」，此翻…: 35 sentence-initial occurrences in dev, roughly 22–25 of them real take-ups, 0 detected, because no entry rule begins with 「 (VERIFIED, `scanner.py` 338–403, 520–526). (b) The four-exegesis cycle 因緣／約教／本迹／觀心, declared once at `p0002a21` and instantiated 56 times (plus 7 closers) in dev; the scanner sees the openers as weak enters and resolves none (VERIFIED). These two produce the outline's shape: 0 nodes in 卷一下 and one leaf of 13,945 characters (55 % of the dev text, 101 of 193 chunks in the Zhiyi project). A density probe shows the cycle is close to specific to the Lotus 文句 (0.16–0.26 openers per 1,000 characters in T1785/T1728/T1705 against 2.21 in T1718 dev); the lemma device is frequent in T1705 (4.86 per 1,000) and nowhere else probed (VERIFIED, regex level).
3. **The measurement cannot see most improvements yet.** Dev what-ifs scored with the merged scorer (VERIFIED, reproduced by the skeptic): promoting the whole-text division to level 1 alone moves S-F1 TP from 1 to 2 of 12 (0.049 → 0.100); promotion plus keying first children on their parent's start line (sdp's convention) gives TP 4, locator-only 6; adding the lemma-gloss units gives TP 9 (P 0.250, R 0.750, F 0.375) and locator-only 11/12. The lemma units alone add only FP against the sdp key (+8 FP, +0 TP) although they fix the outline where it is broken. So: the key convention and the top-level convention decide the ceiling, and precision against the 12-node sdp dev key is not a quality signal (Zhiyi's dev text has ~75–80 structural take-ups).
4. **"One wrong top-level attachment is the main loss" holds for T0262-xu-root and probably the OOD markup diagnostics, not for T1718.** An exact ancestor-consistent mapping (tree edit distance, forgiving one slip) recovers only 4 of 12 dev nodes on T1718 (AC-F1 0.195); on tier2-T0262-xu-root one re-anchor recovers 8 of 11 at t = 1 (AC-F1 0.842) (VERIFIED, independent re-implementation). The resolver re-attachment pivot therefore has a dev ceiling of about 4 of 11 misses.
5. **A scorer defect inverts one hypothesis verdict.** In root-text mode a matched pair counts as TP when the gold node is counted, even when the prediction is not (`score.py` 574–592). model-only-T0262-xu-root's t = 1 S-F1 0.625 rests on 3 uncounted predictions; under "both sides counted" it is 0.308 and tier2's 0.300 is 0.211; under "a matched prediction takes its twin's split" the values stand (VERIFIED). H7's "model-only above hybrid" depends on which rule you pick. A `TP ≤ counted predictions` assertion would have caught it.
6. **The resolver was asked unanswerable questions, not starved by request size.** On dev, 4 of the 10 unanswered root-spans nodes had a one-line admissible range because whole-text parts (正, 流通 and the alternative division) hang under the 序品 pin, and the other 6 were whole-text parts or method items that no span inside 序品 can hold (VERIFIED, request `5e8a5ee8…` lines 199–224). On the pre-registered validation-only build tier 1 produced **no 品 pin at all** (`runs/tier1-T1718-validation`: drafts 0), so every admissible range was the whole sūtra and 16 nodes were skipped (VERIFIED); the devval build, which has the pin, skipped 0. The uncommitted worktree fixes request sizing, windows oversized leaves, allows depth-3 subdivide and declared gaps, and its 57 resolver/e2e tests pass; its rebase onto main is conflict-free (VERIFIED). It does **not** reach the Zhiyi giant leaf: subdivide still selects leaves by root-span length (五列名's root span is six lines) and only childless nodes (VERIFIED, worktree `resolver.py` 953–960).
7. **The Kuiji root-span 1.000 is regular, which is not the same as independent.** 23 of the 29 hybrid starts sit on lines with a surface cue (爾時／佛告／即說呪曰／世尊／而說偈言), and a 17-rule label-to-cue table reproduces 40 of 41 counted spans at t = 0 (VERIFIED). But the table was written with per-node dev feedback, and two Claude runs chose different sides of the same cue on the six spell/verse cuts, so the same-model confound is narrowed to those cuts, not closed. Model-only's 陀羅尼品 tree is isomorphic to the gold below the two commentary-only levels (child counts 2/4/3/3/7 agree) and its bottom-level shallowness is partly `rounds_max = 3` (round 3 still accepted 7 divisions) (VERIFIED).
8. **For Kurt's actual deliverable (right-sized chunks with outline context) the losses are elsewhere.** In the guanjing project 68 % of chunks share their outline label with more than 30 other chunks and only 7 % carry a label shared by ≤ 10; Zhiyi 71 % / 13 %; qixin 0 % / 32 % but 54 % of chunks sit in spans over 2,000 characters (VERIFIED, `runs/project-*/chunk`). Causes, each verified on the text: 8 of guanjing's 12 lemma-not-found cases are one attachment error (又就前序中 resolved as a sibling wrapper, `sim('前序','明其序分') = 0.25`) that drives the anchor's monotone cursor to the last line of the sūtra; 善導's three idioms are absent from the inventory (就X中 entry, 47 hits; unbracketed N、從A下至B已來 items, ~160–190 with ~75 % both ends verbatim in T0365; 廣明X竟 closes, 46); qixin's own divisions are rejected by the doctrinal-list filter (9 rejections, all taken up later as 所言X者 / 云何修行X門). Hand-placed simulations with the real anchor/segment/chunk functions: guanjing giant-leaf share 68 % → 22 %, largest leaf 27 % → 10 % (a ceiling given correct attachment); qixin >2,000-character share 54 % → 13 %.
9. **Out of domain is three problems.** 註 texts (X0268, D8842) state no divisions in formulae but carry explicit structure in markup the pipeline ignores: 1,100 `cb:div type=orig`/`commentary` pairs in X0268, 24 in D8842, and X0268 prints its own kēpàn chart (537 lines; the chart parser already finds 474 items but is gated to whole-text charts) (VERIFIED). Treatises (T1602, T1605, P1573) *are* formula-structured in another dialect: the filter rejects their enumerations (152 of 167 T1602 rejections cite "classifier names kinds"), ~38–43 of 168 have their items taken up in order; P1573 is unpunctuated and its 十法 head verb is not in the inventory. The open T1602 trigger is verified (若X者 conditionals enter one-character items); excluding function-head subjects is dev-neutral, but rewriting `sim()` is not (T1718 dev relies on it for 歎德 → 歎) (VERIFIED by an instrumented dev build). Both E06 failed-cell bugs are confirmed fixed on main.
10. **Robustness.** One local validator error still discards a whole text; the repair pass must run **before** the resolver call (`pipeline.py` 222), not after the validate at 238, or hybrid cells of the T1602 kind keep failing (VERIFIED).

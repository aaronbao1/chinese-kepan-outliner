## 2. Grouping and classification rules (as coded)

* **Cluster** = CBETA 部類 folder below the 部類 top whose descendants hold ≥ 1 root text (底本 部 in the Indian ranges above) and ≥ 1
  commentary (T 經疏／律疏／論疏／諸宗／古逸／疑似 部 or X 釋經／釋律／釋論／諸宗著述 部); every commentary placement attaches to its
  *deepest* such ancestor; a cluster's roots are the root texts not inside a deeper cluster. 76 clusters come from the tree, 18 from an
  explicit manual table (`MANUAL_LINKS` / `MANUAL_WORK_ROOT` in `census.py`) for the cases where CBETA files a root folder and its 疏
  folder as siblings (阿含經疏 → T0014/T0603; 俱舍論疏 → T1558/T1559; 四分律疏 → T1428; 楞嚴 → T0945; 密教疏 direct works; 大日經義釋;
  尊勝陀羅尼; 11 華嚴 digests → T0279; 大般若關法／綱要 → T0220; X0702/X0703/X0709/X0749). Other canons (F, L, P, J, K, G, …) are never
  counted as commentaries. Works CBETA files under two 部類 (淨土 texts) sit in two clusters; totals are over distinct ids.
* **Genre class (title rule)**: 疏 = title contains 疏／文句／玄贊／科; 注 = contains 注／註 and no 疏-marker; other = the rest
  (義記, 述記, 玄義, 宗要, 鈔, 解, 論, 講義 …). Class *other* includes kēpàn-bearing works (T1764 義記 has five 分), so "≥ 1 class-疏"
  is a lower bound on kēpàn coverage and "≥ 1 non-注" an upper bound.

## 6. X0231 and X0584 (R01 F6; `x0231_leaves.py`, `out/x0231_leaves.log`)

X0231 經疏科文 leaves vs T0279 (root, running text 591,189 CJK chars / 27,193 sentence marks) and T1735: **sūtra-span 6,117 (45 %)**,
sutra-elsewhere (incipit occurs in T0279 but outside the monotone interval; 4,348 of them also occur in T1735) 4,380 (32 %),
commentary-internal 2,702 (20 %), unresolved 134 + 1-char 43, count-only stubs 93, no incipit 54. Only 1 sūtra-span leaf precedes the
